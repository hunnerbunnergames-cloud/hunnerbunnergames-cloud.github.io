# HunnerBunner Games — Website & Brand Lock

Date locked: 2026-09-01
Status: **LOCKED / AUTHORITATIVE**

This document records the owner-approved visual and product direction for the HunnerBunner Games studio website. Future implementation should preserve this direction unless the owner explicitly reopens a locked decision.

## 1. Absolute banner lock

The HunnerBunner Games top banner approved in the design conversation is **fully locked** and must be treated as a fixed brand asset, not as a prompt or loose inspiration.

The approved banner is the exact composition with:

- deep navy / near-black horizontal background;
- large handwritten white **HunnerBunner** wordmark on the left;
- widely spaced uppercase **GAMES** beneath it;
- small horizontal accent strokes around the GAMES line;
- centered handwritten phrase: **“Playful. Clever. Always Building Something Fun.”**;
- subtle paw-print details in the background;
- the white/gray HunnerBunner mascot peeking over the lower-right edge;
- pink inner ears, red eyes, confident/mischievous expression, and paws resting on the edge.

**Do not redesign, reinterpret, recolor, re-letter, simplify, replace, or substitute the banner.**

A brown rabbit, cargo-worker rabbit, generic rabbit icon, geometric rabbit mark, monochrome rabbit symbol, or newly generated wordmark is **not** an acceptable replacement for the approved banner.

The banner itself is invariant. Other approved mascot/logo variations may be used elsewhere on the website, loading screens, favicons, small marks, game-studio splash screens, and promotional surfaces.

## 2. Approved logo / mascot family

The brand personality is:

**Playful + confident + slightly mischievous.**

The approved family is:

1. **Primary / banner identity:** the exact locked banner above.
2. **Compact mark:** white/gray bunny head, preserving the same recognizable face, ears, pink inner ears, and confident expression.
3. **Mascot mark:** full-body white/gray bunny, confident and playful rather than enraged.
4. **Horizontal lockup:** mascot/head paired with the same handwritten HunnerBunner lettering style and GAMES treatment.

The mascot may interact with page composition by peeking over, leaning on, or appearing from behind panels. These moments should be restrained and clever rather than gimmicky.

## 3. Approved website visual direction

The owner approved the final full-page mockup after the banner correction. Its overall layout and art direction are the visual target for production.

Core visual rules:

- dark polished indie-studio foundation;
- deep navy / charcoal primary surfaces;
- warm golden-orange accent color;
- soft off-white / parchment-like light sections;
- restrained pink/purple details where useful to support the bunny identity;
- large, readable display typography;
- rounded game cards and polished borders;
- cinematic game artwork allowed to bring its own palette into the studio frame;
- studio identity remains genre-neutral enough to sit beside cozy, puzzle, gritty action, and darker survival games.

The studio should feel professional and durable, not like a Curio Cargo-only website and not excessively cute.

## 4. Homepage composition

The approved homepage should follow the final mockup’s visual rhythm:

1. **Locked HunnerBunner banner at the very top.**
2. Clean primary navigation beneath the banner.
3. Large dark/cinematic hero section.
4. Studio hero messaging and a prominent games CTA.
5. **Our Games** section with Curio Cargo featured more strongly than the development projects.
6. Teaser cards for Project Orchard, Project Iron, and Project Beacon.
7. Light/off-white **Our Story / What We Make** section with the full-body white/gray mascot.
8. Dark structured footer.

The approved homepage hero headline direction is:

**CLEVER GAMES. MADE WITH CARE.**

The canonical studio tagline remains:

> **Small studio. Big worlds. A little mischief.**

These can coexist: the first is the homepage hero headline; the second is the studio tagline and may appear in metadata, About copy, secondary brand surfaces, or future placements.

## 7. About / studio voice

HunnerBunner Games should be presented as an **independent game studio** without publishing an exact team size.

Founder photo is not needed at launch. Focus on the mascot, studio story, and text.

Approved story direction:

> HunnerBunner Games began with a simple impulse: make a game and see where it leads. The studio name grew from “Hunner Bunner,” a longtime nickname from the founder’s wife, and the bunny naturally became part of the studio’s identity. What began as a single experiment has since grown into a collection of projects spanning playful puzzles, cozy worlds, darker adventures, and whatever comes next.

Approved writing style:

- primarily polished and straightforward;
- occasional playful HunnerBunner phrases;
- never force jokes into support, privacy, or functional copy;
- mascot carries much of the personality.

Approved **What We Make** direction:

> We make games with personality. From clever cargo puzzles to cozy worlds, brutal arenas, and darker survival ideas, HunnerBunner Games follows the concept that feels worth building rather than staying inside a single genre.

## 8. Low-maintenance product rules

The website is intentionally designed to require minimal upkeep.

- static site;
- no CMS requirement;
- no database;
- no newsletter at launch;
- no blog/news feed requiring constant updates;
- no contact form;
- no social media links at launch unless real accounts are intentionally created later;
- update primarily for meaningful milestones, releases, project reveals, support changes, or legal changes.

Primary domain:

`hunnerbunnergames.com`

The site should go live before Curio Cargo launches.

## 9. Support / privacy / footer

Support email:

`support@hunnerbunnergames.com`

Support architecture:

- general Support hub;
- Curio Cargo-specific support section/page beneath it.

Privacy architecture:

- one permanent page at `/privacy`;
- general HunnerBunner policy plus game-specific sections beginning with Curio Cargo;
- split into separate policies later only if genuinely required.

Footer should remain minimal and useful. Do **not** add inactive social icons merely because they appeared decoratively in a mockup.

Include:

- HunnerBunner Games branding;
- Games;
- About;
- Support;
- Privacy;
- support email;
- automatic current-year copyright;
- **All rights reserved.**

## 10. Conflict / precedence rule

When implementation details conflict:

1. This lock document controls studio website/brand decisions.
2. The exact approved banner is immutable unless the owner explicitly changes it.
3. The approved final homepage mockup controls overall visual composition and mood.
4. Earlier explicit functional decisions in this document override incidental mockup artifacts (for example inactive social-media icons).
5. Individual game repositories remain authoritative for claims about their games.

Do not silently redesign locked brand elements because a new generation or template looks attractive. If a materially different direction is proposed, show it separately and obtain explicit approval before replacing the locked design.
