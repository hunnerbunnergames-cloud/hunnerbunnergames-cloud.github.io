## Approved Curio Cargo website image

The owner approved the Curio Cargo promotional scene created from the current game concept references as the primary Curio Cargo image for the HunnerBunner Games website.

The approved composition shows the Curio Cargo world as a warm, detailed freight depot / warehouse scene with:

- the zebra mascot as the central recognizable character;
- colorful reinforced cargo crates and unusual curios;
- trucks and warehouse/depot activity;
- recognizable freight such as museum, wildlife, produce, medical, and fossil-style cargo;
- warm evening lighting and the established green / gold / wood / industrial Curio Cargo visual language;
- a playful logistics-adventure feeling rather than generic warehouse stock art.

The production website asset is stored at:

`/assets/art/curio-website.webp.b64`

That file is a web-optimized encoding of the owner-approved promotional artwork. Compression/resizing for web delivery is allowed, but the scene should not be regenerated, substantially reinterpreted, or replaced with unrelated Curio Cargo art without owner approval.

## Usage

Use this approved promotional artwork as the primary Curio Cargo visual on:

- the featured Curio Cargo card on the homepage;
- the Curio Cargo card on `/games/`;
- the hero artwork on `/curio-cargo/`.

Representative gameplay/concept screens may be added elsewhere later, but they should supplement rather than silently replace this primary website image unless the owner explicitly approves a change.

## Important separation

This Curio Cargo game artwork does **not** replace or modify the HunnerBunner Games studio banner. The exact studio banner remains immutable under `Design/00_WEBSITE_AND_BRAND_LOCK.md`.
