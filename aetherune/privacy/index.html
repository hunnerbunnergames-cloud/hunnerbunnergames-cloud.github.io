Summary

Aetherune: Merge Drop is a single-player game. We do not ask for your name, email or any account, and your game progress is stored on your device. The game shows optional rewarded ads and collects crash reports. Those services may process limited device data, as described below.

Information the game stores on your device

Your progress (scores, Salvage, Gems, research, Realms, cosmetics, settings and similar game data) is saved locally on your device. We do not receive or store this data on our servers. Uninstalling the game or clearing its app data deletes it.

Advertising

The game offers optional rewarded video ads (for example, "Watch an ad for +50% Salvage"). Ads are only shown when you choose to watch one. Ads are provided through Unity LevelPlay and its partner ad networks. To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, these providers may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID (for example, the Android Advertising ID);
  • device information such as model, operating system version, language and screen size;
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • ad interaction data (for example, whether an ad was viewed or completed).

These providers process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Unity / LevelPlay: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android device settings (Settings â†’ Google â†’ Ads, or Settings â†’ Privacy â†’ Ads, depending on your device). Where required by law (for example, in the EEA, the UK and certain US states), the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice in the game's Settings.

Crash and performance reports

If the game crashes, it may send an anonymous crash report through Unity Cloud Diagnostics to help us fix bugs. A report can include the device model, operating system version, game version and technical details about the error. It does not include your name or contact information.

In-app purchases

The game does not currently offer in-app purchases. If purchases are added later, payments are processed by Google Play; we never see or store your payment details. This policy will be updated before purchases are introduced.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only with the service providers listed above, for the purposes described.

Data retention and deletion

Game progress stays on your device until you delete it. Advertising and crash data is retained by the respective providers according to their policies. To request deletion of data held by an ad provider, use your device's advertising ID reset and the provider's own request process, or contact us and we will help.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Since we don't hold personal data on our own servers, most requests are handled by resetting your advertising ID or by contacting the providers above. We are happy to help: email hunnerbunnergames@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game changes. The effective date above shows when it was last updated, and significant changes will be announced in the game.
