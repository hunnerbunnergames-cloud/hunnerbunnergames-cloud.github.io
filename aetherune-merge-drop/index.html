Aetherune: Merge Drop

MERGE. SURVIVE. REBUILD.

Aetherune: Merge Drop blends satisfying merge-puzzle gameplay with elemental strategy, escalating survival pressure, and meaningful progression. Build your run, restore the Aether Shield, and uncover a growing world of forgotten technology, strange discoveries, and new realms.

Coming to Google Play

The journey

Build a run. Restore a world.

Every run asks you to adapt under growing pressure while feeding lasting progress back into the world beyond the board.

Elemental Strategy

Merge, adapt, and build around distinct elemental powers.

Survive the Pressure

Short, replayable runs grow more dangerous the longer you endure.

Restore the Nexus

Turn each run into lasting progress as the Aether Shield is restored.

Discover a larger world

Beyond the immediate puzzle lies a fractured fantasy-tech civilization of floating islands, luminous Aether technology, ancient structures, and discoveries waiting beyond the Nexus.

The public site will reveal that world gradually as development progresses.
