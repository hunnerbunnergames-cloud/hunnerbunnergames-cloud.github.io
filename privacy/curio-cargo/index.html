Curio Cargo · Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

How information is handled when you play Curio Cargo, use optional rewarded ads, make Google Play purchases, or contact us for support.

Effective October 7, 2026Hunner Bunner Games LLC
CURIO CARGO CO. Privacy & Ads Player Information Ledger

Hunner Bunner Games LLC (“Hunner Bunner Games,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you play Curio Cargo, use its optional advertising features, make purchases through Google Play, or contact us for support.

Curio Cargo is developed and published by Hunner Bunner Games LLC.

01 Information Stored by Curio Cargo

Curio Cargo stores gameplay information necessary for the game to function. This may include information such as:

  • Game progress
  • Settings and preferences
  • In-game currencies
  • Unlocked content
  • Daily task and streak progress
  • Warehouse and progression state
  • Purchase-related entitlement information
  • Other gameplay save data

This information is primarily stored as game save data associated with your installation of Curio Cargo.

Curio Cargo does not currently require you to create a Hunner Bunner Games account in order to play.

02 Optional Rewarded Advertising

Curio Cargo may offer optional rewarded advertisements.

These advertisements are optional and are not required to access the game’s normal core gameplay.

Curio Cargo uses Unity LevelPlay / ironSource and participating advertising partners to provide and mediate rewarded advertisements.

Advertising services may collect or process information such as:

  • Approximate location
  • Device identifiers or other identifiers
  • Device and operating-system information
  • Application version
  • Interactions with advertisements
  • Advertising performance information
  • Diagnostic or technical information

Advertising providers may use this information for purposes that can include:

  • Providing advertisements
  • Measuring advertising performance
  • Analytics
  • Fraud prevention
  • Security
  • Compliance
  • Improving advertising services

The exact information processed may vary depending on your device, region, privacy choices, and the advertising providers available when an advertisement is requested.

03 Your Advertising and Privacy Choices

Curio Cargo provides in-game privacy controls that allow you to manage optional advertising and certain advertising-related privacy choices.

You may choose to allow or decline optional rewarded advertisements.

Declining optional advertisements does not prevent you from playing Curio Cargo’s core gameplay.

Where applicable, Curio Cargo also provides a “Do Not Sell / Share” privacy control. This setting is intended to communicate applicable privacy preferences to supported advertising services.

You may review or change available privacy choices from the Privacy & Ads section of the game’s Settings menu.

Hunner Bunner Games does not sell personal information for cash.

However, certain advertising-related disclosures or uses of information may be considered a “sale” or “sharing” under some privacy laws. Privacy controls are provided where applicable so players can communicate relevant preferences.

04 In-App Purchases

Curio Cargo may offer optional in-app purchases.

Purchases on Android are processed through Google Play Billing.

Google processes payment information according to Google’s own terms and privacy practices.

Hunner Bunner Games does not receive your full credit-card, debit-card, or other payment-card information.

Curio Cargo may receive purchase-related information necessary to:

  • Verify transactions
  • Confirm purchases
  • Restore eligible purchases
  • Provide purchased game content
  • Prevent duplicate fulfillment or fraud

05 How Information Is Used

Information processed in connection with Curio Cargo may be used to:

  • Operate and maintain the game
  • Store gameplay progress and settings
  • Provide optional rewarded advertisements
  • Process and verify purchases
  • Restore eligible purchases
  • Provide purchased content
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, or misuse
  • Diagnose crashes or technical problems
  • Improve game reliability
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Respond to support requests
  • Respond to privacy requests

06 Third-Party Services

Unity LevelPlay / ironSource

Curio Cargo uses Unity LevelPlay / ironSource for optional rewarded advertising and advertising mediation.

Unity and participating advertising providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and practices.

Google Play

Google Play is used for application distribution and in-app purchase processing.

Google may process information associated with downloads, devices, transactions, purchases, and other platform services according to Google’s own privacy policies and terms.

The data practices of third-party providers are controlled by those providers and may change independently of Hunner Bunner Games.

07 Data Sharing

Hunner Bunner Games does not intentionally disclose personal information except where reasonably necessary to:

  • Operate Curio Cargo
  • Provide requested functionality
  • Work with service providers
  • Deliver optional advertising
  • Process or verify purchases
  • Prevent fraud or abuse
  • Protect the game, its players, or Hunner Bunner Games
  • Comply with applicable laws
  • Respond to valid legal requests

Advertising providers may receive advertising-related information when optional advertising services are used, subject to applicable privacy choices, provider practices, and law.

08 Data Retention

Gameplay information stored locally on your device may remain until:

  • The game data is reset
  • Application storage is cleared
  • The application is uninstalled
  • The operating system removes the data
  • An applicable backup or restore system changes or restores the data

Third-party service providers such as Unity and Google maintain information according to their own retention policies.

Hunner Bunner Games may retain information received directly through support or privacy communications for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • Respond to the request
  • Resolve technical or account-related issues
  • Maintain appropriate business records
  • Comply with legal requirements
  • Resolve disputes

09 Data Deletion and Privacy Requests

You may be able to delete locally stored Curio Cargo game data through your device’s application-data controls or by uninstalling the application.

The exact behavior may depend on your device, operating system, backup settings, or platform services.

For questions about privacy, deletion requests, or other data-rights requests, contact:

Hunner Bunner Games LLCsupport@hunnerbunnergames.com

If a request concerns information controlled by a third-party provider, we may direct you to that provider’s privacy-request process or provide reasonable assistance where appropriate.

10 Security

Hunner Bunner Games takes reasonable measures designed to protect information associated with Curio Cargo.

Third-party services used by the game maintain their own technical and organizational security protections.

Information transmitted to supported advertising, platform, or payment services is handled according to the security practices of those providers.

No electronic system, storage system, or method of transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure, and Hunner Bunner Games cannot guarantee absolute security.

11 Children’s Privacy

Curio Cargo is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed to children.

Hunner Bunner Games does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Curio Cargo does not require players to create a Hunner Bunner Games account or submit personal information directly to Hunner Bunner Games in order to play.

Third-party services used by Curio Cargo, including advertising and platform providers, may process limited device or usage information as described elsewhere in this Privacy Policy and according to their own privacy practices.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that a child has provided personal information directly to Hunner Bunner Games, please contact us at:

support@hunnerbunnergames.com

We will review the matter and take appropriate action where necessary.

12 Changes to This Privacy Policy

Hunner Bunner Games may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Changes may be made to reflect:

  • Changes to Curio Cargo
  • Changes to third-party services
  • Changes to advertising or purchasing systems
  • Changes to privacy practices
  • Changes to applicable laws or regulations

When material changes are made, the Effective Date at the top of this Privacy Policy will be updated.
