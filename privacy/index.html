Privacy at HunnerBunner Games

This page is the permanent home for general HunnerBunner Games privacy information and game-specific privacy sections.

Website privacy

Last updated: September 1, 2026.

The HunnerBunner Games website is designed as a low-maintenance static website. It does not require an account, does not provide a contact form, and does not currently include a newsletter or social-media tracking feed.

If you email support@hunnerbunnergames.com, the information you choose to send is used to respond to your message and provide support.

Curio Cargo

Curio Cargo is not yet publicly released. Its final game-specific privacy disclosures will be published in this section before public availability, once the release integrations and data practices are finalized.

This pre-release section is intentionally not a substitute for the final Google Play data-safety and game privacy disclosures.

Questions
