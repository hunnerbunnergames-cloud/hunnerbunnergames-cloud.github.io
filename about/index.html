About HunnerBunner Games

Games with personality.

HunnerBunner Games is an independent game studio following the ideas that feel worth building — wherever those ideas happen to lead.

Our story

How it started

HunnerBunner Games began with a simple impulse: make a game and see where it leads.

The studio name grew from “Hunner Bunner,” a longtime nickname from the founder’s wife, and the bunny naturally became part of the studio’s identity.

What began as a single experiment has since grown into a collection of projects spanning playful puzzles, cozy worlds, darker adventures, and whatever comes next.

What we make

Games with personality.

From clever cargo puzzles to cozy worlds, brutal arenas, and darker survival ideas, HunnerBunner Games follows the concept that feels worth building rather than staying inside a single genre.

That variety is deliberate: the studio identity stays consistent while each game gets room to become its own world.

Small studio. Big worlds.A little mischief.

Studio philosophy

Playful. Clever. Always building something fun.

We aim for games that feel polished, readable, and worth coming back to — without tying the studio to one genre or visual style.
